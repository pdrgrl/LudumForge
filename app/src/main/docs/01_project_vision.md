# 01. Project Vision: LudumForge

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Game Jams are intense, high-pressure events. Teams often struggle with:
1.  **Scope Creep:** Trying to do too much in too little time.
2.  **Organization:** Losing track of who is doing what.
3.  **Deadlines:** Realizing too late that the project won't be finished.
4.  **Planning:** Spending too much valuable jam time creating a schedule.

## 💡 The Solution
**LudumForge** acts as an AI-powered co-pilot for Game Jams. By automating the planning phase and providing real-time "panic management," it allows developers to focus on what matters: **making the game.**

## 🌟 Key Objectives
- **Automated Planning:** Convert a raw game idea into a structured, realistic roadmap in seconds.
- **Survival Guidance:** Protect the MVP (Minimum Viable Product) through the "Panic Button."
- **Seamless Collaboration:** Ensure the team stays synchronized, whether online or offline.
- **Resource Management:** Provide a clear overview of Art, Code, and Audio progress.

## 👤 Target Audience
- Indie Game Developers.
- Game Jam Enthusiasts.
- Student Teams in creative marathons.
- Solo Developers looking for organizational support.

## 🚀 Impact
LudumForge aims to reduce the "failure to submit" rate in Game Jams by ensuring projects stay within scope and teams stay motivated through clear, achievable milestones.
