# 13. Feature Extensions: LudumForge

Future ideas to expand the LudumForge ecosystem.

## 🚀 Near Future
- **Export to PM Tools:** Export the AI roadmap directly to Trello, Jira, or Notion.
- **Voice-to-Task:** Add tasks by speaking ("Add a task to fix the jump bug").
- **Asset Library Integration:** Suggest free assets (from Kenney.nl or OpenGameArt) based on the AI roadmap.

## 🎨 Creative Tools
- **Team Mood Tracker:** A quick daily check-in to see team morale during the jam.
- **AI Sprite/UI Generator:** Integration with Stable Diffusion or similar for rapid placeholder art generation.
- **Design Document Generator:** Generate a full GDD (Game Design Document) based on the project roadmap.

## 📈 Analytics & Community
- **Global Leaderboards:** Fun stats like "Most Efficient Team" or "Least Panic Buttons Pressed."
- **Post-Jam Portfolio:** Generate a webpage summarizing the jam progress, tasks completed, and final game link.
- **Jam History:** Detailed timeline of every project created in LudumForge.
